# Imagens de demonstração · Axo Image Studio

Três imagens quadradas para acompanhar a apresentação do projeto no LinkedIn.

Estas imagens foram criadas com o gerador de imagens integrado desta conversa. São exemplos visuais para apresentação; não são resultados de uma execução do Axo Image Studio, do Forge, do SDXL ou do Anima. Para demonstrar resultados do seu projeto, use as descrições abaixo no Colab e publique também os parâmetros reais da geração.

## Prompts utilizados

As especificações completas abaixo foram enviadas ao gerador integrado. Para testar no Axo, use as descrições de cena, sujeito, estilo e luz como ponto de partida; o modelo e a seed podem produzir resultados diferentes.

### Mascote 3D

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square demonstration artwork for a LinkedIn carousel about Axo Image Studio, a creative image workspace by Pedroltz.
Primary request: a beautiful polished 3D character illustration of a small pale pink axolotl sitting atop a vintage compact desktop computer, with the animal clearly the protagonist.
Scene/backdrop: a cozy dark creative studio, uncluttered, one compact retro cream computer on a desk, subtly glowing screen with abstract soft color shapes, no interface or text. Subject: adorable axolotl with anatomically recognizable external feathery gills, tiny rounded feet, expressive black eyes and a gentle smile, leaning forward with curiosity. Style/medium: cinematic high-end stylized 3D render, tactile handcrafted clay-like finish, soft subsurface scattering, finely detailed feathery pink gills, realistic textured plastic on the computer. Composition: square 1:1 full-bleed artwork, medium shot, centered clear silhouette, complete axolotl and computer visible, comfortable margins, slightly low three-quarter camera angle. Lighting/mood: soft coral key light, cool teal rim light, warm desk glow, calm imaginative atmosphere. Color palette: pale pink, dusty coral, teal highlights, deep charcoal background. Constraints: one axolotl and one computer only, clean distinctive illustration with no text, no watermark, no branding, no collage, no claim that it is a generated result from another application.
```

### Fantasia ilustrada

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: square demonstration artwork for a LinkedIn carousel showcasing creative image ideas.
Primary request: an exquisite anime-inspired fantasy illustration of a little pale pink axolotl discovering a luminous miniature garden inside an abandoned glass greenhouse.
Scene/backdrop: a richly painted yet carefully composed greenhouse, lush emerald foliage, delicate pink blossoms, turquoise glowing water and a winding stone path reflected in a shallow pool. Subject: one charming small pink axolotl in the foreground water, with three feathery external gill branches on each side of its head, tiny rounded feet, curious black eyes. Give it a recognizable silhouette and anatomically plausible body. Style/medium: accomplished hand-painted anime background and refined storybook illustration, expressive precise lines, painterly foliage, nuanced light, no imitation of a named artist. Composition: square 1:1, full-bleed, subject prominently framed in lower foreground, greenhouse arches lead toward the garden, foreground/midground/background depth, visual breathing room, no split panels. Lighting/mood: gentle magical dusk, turquoise reflections and warm peach light through glass, curious and peaceful. Color palette: coral pink, teal, emerald, golden peach. Constraints: one axolotl only, no humans, no writing, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no advertising banner.
```

### Fotografia subaquática

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square demonstration artwork for a LinkedIn carousel showing a photographic visual direction.
Primary request: an exceptionally convincing underwater macro photograph of a pale pink leucistic axolotl in a clean freshwater habitat.
Scene/backdrop: freshwater aquarium habitat with smooth dark river stones, soft moss and out-of-focus aquatic plants, clean clear water. Subject: a single axolotl, anatomically accurate with three delicate branching external gills on each side of its head, broad rounded head, two natural dark eyes, translucent pale pink skin with tiny realistic texture details, small natural limbs and a finned tail; no humanized pose or expression. Composition/framing: square 1:1 full-bleed photographic frame, eye-level three-quarter close-up, axolotl head and forelimbs clearly visible, body gently receding, subtle shallow depth of field, natural lens rendering, do not crop gill tips. Lighting/mood: soft diffuse underwater daylight, gentle teal environmental reflections and warm pink gills, calm naturalistic editorial wildlife photography. Materials/textures: believable translucent skin and fine gill filaments, crisp focus on eyes, organically irregular river stones. Constraints: photograph-like rather than 3D/cartoon, no exaggerated grin, no extra eyes/limbs/gills, no dramatic bubbles covering animal, no text, no watermark, no collage, no UI.
```
